# Contributing Guidelines

Thank you for considering contributing to this project! Please follow the guidelines below to maintain a clean and organised workflow.

## How to Contribute
1. **Fork the repository** to your GitHub account.
2. **Create a new branch** for each change (example: `feature/new-ui`, `fix/bug-1`).
3. **Write clear commit messages** explaining what you changed.
4. **Test your changes** before submitting.
5. **Create a Pull Request** describing:
   - What you changed
   - Why you changed it
6. Be respectful and collaborative while discussing issues and PRs.

We appreciate your contributions!
